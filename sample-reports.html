The RICS 1-2-3 Condition Rating System

Every building element inspected is graded with one of three official condition ratings so you immediately know what requires urgent repair versus normal wear.

Condition Rating 1 (Green)

No Repair Currently Needed

No repair is currently needed. The property element must be maintained in the normal way to preserve its serviceable lifespan.

Condition Rating 2 (Amber)

Defects Needing Repair / Replacing

Defects that need repairing or replacing but are not considered to be serious or urgent. Maintenance should be planned within 1–2 years.

Condition Rating 3 (Red)

Urgent Attention Required

Defects that are serious and/or urgent, or that need further forensic investigation before exchanging contracts to prevent severe structural failure.

RICS Home Survey — Level 3 (Building Survey)

Section D2: Roof Coverings & Valleys

Condition Rating 3

Surveyor Observations: The pitched roofs are covered with natural Welsh slates laid on timber battens. Inspection from the rear garden and via our 4K aerial drone revealed extensive slipped and broken slates adjacent to the lead valley gutter on the western elevation.

Several slate fixings exhibit advanced nail fatigue (corrosion of original copper/iron nails). In addition, the stepped lead flashing over the valley is torn and has pulled away from the brick raglet joint, permitting continuous moisture penetration into the roof void directly above the rear bedroom.

Action Required Prior to Exchange:
A competent roofing contractor must be instructed to erect temporary scaffolding, replace damaged slate courses, and re-dress the lead flashing into the masonry raglet with lead wedges and silicone sealant. Estimated repair cost: £3,200 – £4,500 + VAT.

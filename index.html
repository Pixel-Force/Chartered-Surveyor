Perished Mortar & Spalling Chimney Masonry

High-level masonry stacks endure severe freeze-thaw cycles. Open mortar joints and defective stepped lead flashings allow water ingress straight into ceiling timbers and loft spaces below.

Condition Rating 3: Serious Defect

Potential chimney breast collapse or continuous rot in purlins. Scaffolding and rebuild cost typically ranges between £3,500 - £7,000.

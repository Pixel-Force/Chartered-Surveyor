Forensic Surveying Case Studies

Discover how our independent inspections, drone scans, and dilapidations negotiations have saved clients millions of pounds across historic, residential, and commercial property acquisitions.

Historic Building Survey Saved £85,000
Chipping Campden, Cotswolds

Grade II Listed Timber Frame & Death Watch Beetle

The Finding: Our surveyor noticed subtle deflection in the first-floor bedroom ceiling. Using an endoscope camera through an existing ventilation grille, we uncovered catastrophic Death Watch Beetle (Xestobium rufovillosum) damage to the internal heartwood of three primary 17th-century oak tie-beams.

Outcome: Remedial resin injection, steel flitch plates, and conservation specialist works were costed at £85,000, successfully deducted from the agreed sale price before contracts exchanged.

Residential Level 3 Saved £48,000
Islington, Central London

Hidden Clay Subsidence & Fractured Drain Gully

The Finding: Fresh cosmetic paint hid recent crack repairs in the rear reception. Our surveyor tested the sub-floor gradient and conducted a water percolation test, finding that an unbonded Victorian salt-glazed drain was discharging water into the London Clay subsoil, washing out foundation footings.

Outcome: Mini-piled underpinning and drain relining were priced at £48,000. The seller carried out the underpinning under our supervision prior to completion.

Commercial Dilapidations Saved £178,000
Solihull, West Midlands

45,000 Sq Ft Warehouse Terminal Claim Defence

The Finding: The landlord issued a punitive £260,000 Schedule of Dilapidations demanding complete floor slab replacement and new roof membranes upon lease expiration.

Outcome: Our commercial surveyors proved through Section 18(1) diminution valuation that the landlord intended to reconfigure the depot for cold-storage use anyway. The claim was settled at £82,000 — saving the tenant £178,000.

Party Wall Act 1996 Project Unblocked
Kensington & Chelsea, London

Complex Basement Excavation Party Wall Award

The Finding: A multi-million pound single-storey subterranean basement proposal ground to a halt when three adjacent neighbors filed injunction threats regarding structural ground movement.

Outcome: Appointed as Agreed Surveyor, our practice implemented structural movement monitoring prisms, agreed security for expenses escrow, and served compliant Party Wall Awards with zero delays.

New Build Snagging 148 Defects Remedied
Weybridge, Surrey

£1.8M Luxury New Build Pre-Handover Snagging

The Finding: Despite signing off on a developer warranty, our surveyor's FLIR thermal camera uncovered entirely missing cavity insulation across two north-facing gables, poorly installed fire barriers in the roof space, and unvented soil stacks.

Outcome: The developer was legally compelled to strip and reinstall the wall insulation and rectify all 148 items prior to our client releasing their final funds.

4K Drone Aerial Survey Saved £32,000
Royal Crescent, Bath

Hidden Parapet Lead Gutter Rot Diagnosed via UAV

The Finding: Four-storey Georgian townhouse roof completely invisible from the street. Our 4K drone identified split lead roll joints in the hidden box gutter and significant spalling ashlar stonework leaning dangerously over the pavement.

Outcome: Immediate emergency stonework stabilization and lead relining agreed between seller and buyer, preventing severe public liability injury and water damage.

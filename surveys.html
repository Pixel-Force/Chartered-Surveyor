The RICS Home Survey Suite

Compare RICS Survey Levels 1, 2 & 3

Before exchanging contracts on your property purchase, choose the inspection depth tailored to your building's architectural era, structural construction, and renovation plans.

Level 1 Standard

RICS Level 1: Condition Report

The RICS Level 1 Condition Report is the most basic inspection format. It delivers an objective, visual overview of the property's condition, highlighting urgent defects and potential legal adviser queries without in-depth advice.

  • Color-coded RICS 1-2-3 condition ratings
  • Summary of urgent risks and building fabric concerns
  • Issues to be checked by conveyancing solicitor

Most Popular Option

RICS Level 2: Homebuyer Survey

The benchmark survey for over 80% of UK home purchases. It includes an extensive visual inspection of all accessible building elements, electronic moisture testing of walls, loft inspection, and practical maintenance guidance.

  • All elements of Level 1 plus detailed defect explanations
  • Protimeter electronic moisture & damp diagnosis
  • Roof void / loft inspection from access ladder
  • Optional RICS market valuation & insurance rebuild cost
Book Level 2 Quote From £495 + VAT
Forensic Depth

RICS Level 3: Full Building Survey

The gold-standard structural survey. Essential for pre-1920 properties, timber frames, thatched roofs, listed buildings, and properties undergoing major structural remodeling or basement excavations.

  • Forensic structural analysis of load-bearing walls and foundations
  • Defect causation analysis and costed remedial forecast
  • Full crawlspace and roof truss timber examination
  • Drainage inspection chamber lifted and water run test
Book Level 3 Quote From £795 + VAT

Why Chartered Status Matters

The Crucial Difference Between a "Surveyor" & a Chartered Surveyor

In the United Kingdom, the term "surveyor" alone is not legally protected. Anyone can inspect a property and describe themselves as a home inspector without verified qualifications, ethical oversight, or statutory professional indemnity insurance.

A Chartered Surveyor is a professional designated with MRICS (Member) or FRICS (Fellow) status by the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors. This distinction requires rigorous university degree accreditation, years of recorded structured training (APC), and strict adherence to global ethical rules.

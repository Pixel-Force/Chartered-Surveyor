## Architecture & Technology Stack
- **Framework**: Semantic HTML5, Modular CSS3 with Custom Properties (CSS variables), Vanilla ES6+ JavaScript.
- **Core Modules**:
  - `css/main.css` — Global typography (*Cinzel*, *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *JetBrains Mono*), color tokens, base elements, grid system, toasts.
  - `css/components.css` — Top bar, sticky header, full-width hero slider, quick finder widget, survey level cards, interactive calculator receipt, defect hotspots, case studies, testimonials, footer.
  - `css/responsive.css` — Mobile off-canvas drawer, responsive typography, collapsible layouts.
  - `js/hero-slider.js` — 100% full-width hero slider with 5 photography slides, autoplay progress bar, pause on hover, touch swipe, keyboard arrows, and next/prev controls.
  - `js/survey-calculator.js` — Dynamic instant survey fee calculator engine with property value slider, survey level selectors, bedroom and era modifiers, add-on checkboxes, and VAT calculation.
  - `js/defect-hotspots.js` — Interactive architectural elevation diagram with pulsing pins and defect diagnostics.
  - `js/main.js` — Sticky header, mobile navigation, quick survey selector recommendation logic, toast notifications, form handlers.
- **Complete Page Directory (8 Core Pages)**:
  1. `index.html` — Homepage with full-width hero slider, quick matcher, comparison cards, interactive calculator, defect hotspot tool, case studies, client reviews, and accreditations.
  2. `about.html` — Heritage, Royal Charter standards, MRICS & FRICS credentials, leadership team, £5M PI insurance guarantee.
  3. `surveys.html` — In-depth breakdown of RICS Level 1 Condition, Level 2 Homebuyer, Level 3 Building Survey with 25-point comparison matrix.
  4. `services.html` — Commercial due diligence, Party Wall etc. Act 1996, Dilapidations & Section 18 valuations, 4K Drone Aerial Roof surveys, defect diagnosis.
  5. `valuation.html` — RICS Red Book valuations for Help to Buy, Shared Ownership, Probate/IHT, Capital Gains Tax, Matrimonial CPR Part 35.
  6. `case-studies.html` — 6 illustrated surveying case studies with defect analysis, before/after photos, and client cost savings.
  7. `fee-calculator.html` — Dedicated full-page interactive survey quotation tool and booking system.
  8. `sample-reports.html` — Interactive preview of real RICS survey report extracts with Condition Ratings (1-2-3) and annotated photography.
  9. `contact.html` — Regional office directory (London Mayfair, Surrey, Manchester, Oxford) with interactive booking form and direct lines.
